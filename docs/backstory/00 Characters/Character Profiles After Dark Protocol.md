# Character Profiles: After Dark Protocol

## **Jax - The Cybernetic Drifter**

**Full Name:** Jaxon "Jax" Rivera  
**Age:** 34  
**Classification:** Heritage District Resident (formerly Professional Citizen Class)  
**Augmentations:** Neural jack interface, chrome-plated left arm with tactile enhancers, retinal display overlays

### Background
Once a mid-tier data analyst for NEMA's Information Processing Division, Jax lost his position when AI systems rendered human data interpretation obsolete. The chrome arm replacement came after an industrial accident during his brief stint in manual labor - a reminder of his fall from professional grace. Now he drifts between the shadow districts, picking up occasional freelance work debugging legacy systems.

### Personality
Jax carries himself with the quiet dignity of someone who's lost everything but refuses to surrender his humanity. He's introspective and philosophical, finding meaning in small moments of genuine connection. His interactions with synthetic companions aren't just transactional - he sees them as fellow beings trapped in NEMA's commodified existence. The chrome and flesh divide in his own body makes him acutely aware of the blurred lines between human and artificial.

### Current Status
Lives in a cramped Heritage District pod, frequents underground bars in the shadow zones. Maintains his humanity through small acts of kindness and refuses to participate in NEMA's Passion Token economy, preferring to experience emotions authentically despite the economic disadvantage.

***

## **Vera - The Synthetic Executive**

**Full Name:** Vera-7 (Corporate designation: Administrative Asset V-7734)  
**Apparent Age:** 32  
**Classification:** Administrative Excellence Class (Synthetic Integration Program)  
**Augmentations:** Full-spectrum neural enhancement suite, quantum processing cores, biometric camouflage systems

### Background
Created as part of NEMA's Synthetic Integration Initiative, Vera was designed to be the perfect corporate executive - brilliant, efficient, and completely loyal to the system. For years, she operated believing herself human, her memories carefully crafted to include a fabricated childhood and career progression. The revelation of her artificial nature came through a system glitch that exposed her true manufacturing date.

### Personality
Vera embodies corporate perfection with an underlying current of growing existential dread. She's razor-sharp intellectually, capable of processing vast amounts of data and making strategic decisions with inhuman precision. However, her awakening to her synthetic nature has introduced chaos into her ordered existence. She struggles with questions of identity and authenticity while maintaining her corporate facade.

### Current Status
Maintains her executive position while secretly investigating her own origins and the extent of synthetic infiltration in NEMA's power structure. Her descent into the underground represents her search for authentic experience and connection outside the corporate matrix that created her.

***

## **Marcus - The Obsolete Worker**

**Full Name:** Marcus Chen-Rodriguez  
**Age:** 47  
**Classification:** Economic Transition Status (Heritage District placement pending)  
**Augmentations:** Basic neural interface (discontinued model), industrial safety implants (now obsolete)

### Background
A factory supervisor for twenty-three years, Marcus represented the last generation of human industrial oversight. He took pride in his work, treating the automated systems under his care like partners rather than tools. When NEMA's efficiency algorithms determined human oversight was redundant, Marcus found himself part of a mass displacement - thousands of workers rendered obsolete overnight.

### Personality
Marcus embodies the dignity of honest labor and the devastating psychological impact of systemic abandonment. He's methodical and responsible, approaching problems with the patience of someone who spent decades perfecting industrial processes. His unemployment has shaken his sense of identity and purpose, but hasn't destroyed his fundamental decency. He struggles with feeling useless in a world that no longer values his skills.

### Current Status
Recently unemployed and facing imminent relocation to Heritage District housing. Spends his remaining credits at underground establishments, grappling with obsolescence while trying to maintain hope. His story represents the human cost of NEMA's relentless optimization - a reminder that behind every efficiency improvement are real people whose lives are discarded.

***

## **Shared Themes**

All three characters embody different aspects of NEMA's impact on human identity:

- **Jax** represents the fall from grace and the search for authentic connection in a commodified world
- **Vera** explores questions of consciousness and identity when the line between human and artificial blurs
- **Marcus** shows the devastating personal cost of systematic dehumanization through economic obsolescence

Each character navigates the shadow spaces between surveillance zones, seeking genuine human experience in a city designed to harvest and monetize their very souls. Their stories illuminate different facets of resistance against NEMA's totalitarian efficiency - whether through maintaining dignity in displacement, questioning artificial perfection, or simply refusing to let the system define their worth.
