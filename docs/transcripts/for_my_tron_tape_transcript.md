# AUDIO TRANSCRIPT // "FOR MY TRON // 1988 ETERNAL"
### **Cassette Specifications:** Maxell XLII-S 90 // High Bias // Chrome Position // Dolby B NR Enabled.
### **Subject:** Audio mix diary left in the cockpit compartment of the scout vessel *Vigil-88* by Dr. Jennifer Ruiz for Engineer Daak (Pre-conversion Syntho-Tron 5000).

---

## 📻 SIDE A: THE LIGHT OF AETHELGARD

* **[00:00 - 00:15]**  
  * **Audio Direction:** Faint mechanical click of the cassette deck head engaging. Low, warm analog tape hiss. Faint hum of Aethelgard-4’s secondary coolant pump in the background. A soft, fingerstyle Spanish classical guitar begins playing a gentle bossa nova melody.
  
* **[00:15]**  
  * **Jen (VO):** *(Laughs softly, close to the microphone. Her voice is warm, carrying a gentle Andalusian lilt)*  
    "Testing... testing... is this thing on? Ah, the little red light is glowing. Okay. This is Doctor Jennifer Ruiz, officially cataloging for the archives that Chief Engineer Daak is eighty percent cold tungsten and twenty percent sentimental fool.  
    
    *(Pause, her smile audible)*  
    
    I know you're going to roll your eyes when you hear this, Daak. You’ll probably say analog tape has too much harmonic distortion. But you also said it has warmth. That it remembers the soul of the microphone. So... I’m leaving a piece of my soul in here for you."

* **[01:10]**  
  * **Audio Direction:** The guitar music swells slightly, then transitions into a slower, melancholic flamenco rhythm. The sound of rain falling against window glass (recorded on shore leave in Greenwich) mixes with the music.

* **[01:30]**  
  * **Jen (VO):** *(Whispering, as if lying next to him in the dark)*  
    "Entry 42. It’s three in the morning, and the station is completely quiet. You think I’m recording stellar spectral data on the terminal. But really, I’m just holding the microphone near your chest while you sleep. Listen...  
    
    *(A low, steady human heartbeat plays under the tape hiss for five seconds)*  
    
    ...that’s you, Daak. That's the hearth of this entire station. You frown when you're awake, always calculating orbital decay. But when you sleep, that stubborn crease between your brows disappears, and I see the boy who used to stare at Saturn through a cardboard telescope in London. Never stop looking at the stars with me. I love you with every byte of my consciousness."

* **[02:45]**  
  * **Audio Direction:** The music fades down. The ambient hum of the station becomes slightly more prominent, representing the high-altitude orbit.

* **[03:00]**  
  * **Jen (VO):** *(Smiling, voice energetic and light)*  
    "Remember Seville, Daak? The rain in the Cathedral courtyard? You looked so ridiculous in that soaked uniform, trying to explain that the sunset was just atmospheric refraction. But your hands were so warm when you took mine. Let's promise. When this tour ends next year, we are leaving the telemetry bays. We are going to New Arcadia. We’ll build the greenhouse. I’ll plant the rosemary, and you can spend all your credits on obsolete Tektronix oscilloscopes. I won't even yell at you. Well... maybe a little. But then I'll just kiss you until you stop arguing."

## 🌌 SIDE B: THE WINE-DARK SEA

* **[00:00 - 00:20]**  
  * **Audio Direction:** Loud click of the tape turning over. Deep, crackling vinyl static fading in. The sound of a solo classical guitar playing a slow, weeping melody in a minor key. Faint, rhythmic static pulses, mimicking a deep-space radio transmission.

* **[00:20]**  
  * **Jen (VO):** *(Her voice is softer, thicker with unshed tears, recorded in the final days of the mandate)*  
    "They’re calling up the reserve assets, Daak. The rumors are true. The Directorate... they want to take you. They want to put your mind in the tungsten hull. But they don't understand. They think they can optimize you. They think a machine doesn't need to love.  
    
    *(A shaky breath, her voice cracking slightly)*  
    
    I wrote this down for you. From the books we read. Hear my voice when the cold void gets too loud..."

* **[01:15]**  
  * **Jen (VO):** *(Reciting with fierce, poetic devotion, tears in her voice)*  
    "No matter how many astronomical units lie between us, my love...  
    Remember Odysseus always found his way back across the wine-dark sea.  
    Let the solar winds blow, let the telemetry sensors fail.  
    Your soul is bound to mine, etched in permanent silicon,  
    And no military format command can ever erase what we built.  
    I will keep the beacon burning.  
    Keep the tubes warm, my Tron."

* **[02:10]**  
  * **Audio Direction:** The guitar music becomes distant, drowned out by a rising swell of electronic carrier wave hum (1420 MHz).

* **[02:25]**  
  * **Jen (VO):** *(Recording from the launch platform gantry, her voice echoing slightly in the massive steel hangar. The roar of rocket engines is heard far in the distance)*  
    "Daak! Can you hear me through the gantry lines? The mechs are sealing the viewport. They think they've replaced your heart with static RAM. But they're wrong! Your hand was warm on my cheek. I'm holding your wedding ring right now. Do you hear me, Tron? Odysseus came home! Ten years across the dark, he still came home! You come home to me!  
    
    *(Weeping, desperately)*  
    
    Keep the tubes warm, my love... please... keep them warm..."

* **[03:20]**  
  * **Audio Direction:** The rocket engines roar with deafening power. The classical guitar melody plays one final, unresolved chord. The signal undergoes a severe digital distortion spike, transitioning into a flat, steady carrier wave hum that slowly fades into empty analog tape hiss. A final sharp click as the cassette deck auto-stops.
