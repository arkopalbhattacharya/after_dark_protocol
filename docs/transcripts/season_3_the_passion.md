### EPISODE 21: "NEON SHADOWS & WARM SHEETS"

### EPISODE 22: "THE SCAR ACROSS YOUR RIBS"
**Location**: Shower & Bunk Enclave.

**JENNIFER**: (Her damp fingers tracing a long, pale scar across Daak’s left ribcage) Where did this come from? You never told me.

**DAAK**: (Looking down at her tenderly) A pressure manifold exploded on the Titan-7 freight dock twelve years ago. Shrapnel tore through the bulkhead. The surgeons patched the lung with titanium mesh.

**JENNIFER**: (Kisses the jagged line of the scar softly, resting her cheek against his ribcage) Does it still hurt?

**DAAK**: Only when the station drops barometric pressure. But when you touch it... it feels like it was put there just so you could kiss it better.

**JENNIFER**: (Smiles up at him through her lashes, sliding her hands around his neck) You’re a terrible flatterer, Engineer. Now come back under the duvet before you catch a chill.

### EPISODE 24: "FEARS IN THE QUIET HOURS"

**JENNIFER**: (Staring at the ceiling, her fingers restlessly playing with the silver chain around Daak’s neck) Daak? Are you awake?

**DAAK**: (Opens his eyes, immediately sensing the shift in her mood, turning onto his side to face her) Always for you. What is it, mi vida?

### EPISODE 28: "CHILDHOOD MEMORIES ON THE PILLOW"
**Location**: Bunk 04, 03:45 UTC.

**DAAK**: When I was seven in East London, I built a telescope out of cardboard tubes and magnifying glasses. I tried to see the rings of Saturn from my bedroom window.

**JENNIFER**: (Resting her chin on his chest, smiling softly) Did you see them?

**DAAK**: Just a blurry smudge of light. But I sat on that cold windowsill until my fingers were blue, because I knew there was something vast and perfect out there.

**JENNIFER**: (Softly) And now you’re out here.

**DAAK**: (Kisses her forehead) And the only vast, perfect thing I care about is lying in my arms.
